## Reusable technical findings (cost real debugging time)

1. **UE Interchange `import_offset_rotation` is silently ignored unless
   `common_meshes_properties.bake_meshes = True`.** With `bake_meshes = False` the
   import produced byte-identical bounds before and after setting `roll = -90`. If an
   offset rotation appears to do nothing, check `bake_meshes` first.
2. **glTF up-axis must be verified from mesh bounds, not assumed.** Interchange assumes
   Y-up. This source is Z-up (stone base Z 0–9.7 m, roof trim Z 13.2–17.9 m, gate sign
   on the front face at Y ≈ -3 m). Detection rule: if the largest extent is Z and the
   roof nodes sit at high Z, the file is Z-up. Correcting it needs `bake_meshes = True`
   plus `import_offset_rotation = roll -90°` (same as the GreatWestGate import).
3. **Interchange collapses multi-node mesh instances into one merged mesh.** The roof
   tile (6M tris) is referenced by 9 nodes; the imported `Roof_Upper_Front` asset
   contains all 9 sections already positioned (bounds matched the union of the 9 node
   transforms exactly). No manual instancing needed — do not assume one asset per node.
4. **`mesh.get_num_triangles(0)` returns the Nanite fallback, not source LOD0** (74,606
   for a 6,000,000-triangle mesh; 9,862 after baking). Verify fidelity from the .uasset
   size and the glTF index accessor instead.
5. **`unreal.AutomationLibrary.take_high_res_screenshot` is async and only flushes after
   the Python script returns.** One screenshot per script run; a second camera move in
   the same run discards the first frame.

## Reusable technical findings (new, beyond the earlier list)

6. **This V2 model family has no instancing** — every mesh is referenced by exactly one
   node, all at identity under one `world` root. So the parts need no reassembly; the
   earlier 9-node roof collapse does not apply here. Always decode the GLB before
   choosing flags rather than assuming the previous model's shape.
7. **Determine which way a gate faces from the plaque mesh, not by eye.** `GNG_PLAQUE`
   is the only Y-asymmetric part. Source Y −3.83..−3.61 maps to UE Y **+361..+383**, so
   the UE axis map is `(x, −y, z) × 100` and the facade faces **+Y** — the same side as
   the level's sun. Two captures shot from −Y were wasted on a backlit back view.
8. **A mesh can be a one-sided soffit with no top surface — measure it, do not eyeball
   it.** Area-weighted face orientation from the GLB gave `GNG_ROOF` 0.30% up / 83.65%
   down, while `GNG_STONE`/`GNG_WOOD` were symmetric at ~25%/25%. The symmetry of the
   closed meshes is what makes the roof result trustworthy. Symptom to watch for: an
   object that renders as thin edge slivers from above and disappears when the parts
   around it are hidden. Fix belongs in the 3D pipeline, not at import.
9. **Two-sided material is the only in-engine stopgap for a missing top surface.**
   `material.set_editor_property("two_sided", True)` + `save_loaded_asset` makes the
   soffit render from above. It is a stopgap, not a fix, and a re-import regenerates the
   material and clears it.
