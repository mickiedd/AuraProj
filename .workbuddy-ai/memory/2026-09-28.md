### The finding that closes an open question — the gate route

**It is a footprint conflict, not a collision bug.** `BP_Wenmingmen` is spawned at
`(180000, 121500)` across a 6 m corridor. Declared footprint 65 × 35 m; placed Blueprint
6500.0 × 2806.9 × 2199.0 cm. The detour's furthest-east point is **3293 cm** from the road centre;
gate half-width 3250 cm + UE default nav agent radius 35 cm = **3285 cm** — an 8 cm agreement, so
the path is rounding the gate's own outer wall. The package models a **closed** recessed double
timber door with complex collision on structural meshes, and the route's own start
`(180000,120500)` sits *inside* the footprint. **The query is unsatisfiable while the doors are
shut** — so `CANTON-M05-004` should be re-worded from "diagnose gate opening and collision" to a
chosen resolution (move the gate, open a passage, end the route at the threshold, or waive).

Reusable trick: **a nav detour's apex is a measurement.** Compare it against the offending asset's
half-width plus agent radius and it tells you *what* the path went around — no viewport needed.
