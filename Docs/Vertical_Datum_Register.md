# Vertical datum and elevation register — 2026-09-24

**Blocked for historical height production.** No site-specific bare-earth survey, historic contour sheet or archaeological ground level with a documented datum has been acquired. `Data/Elevation_Constraints.csv` contains a schema only; no height has been inferred from gate mesh dimensions.

The [Copernicus GLO-30 product handbook](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/sites/default/files/media/files/2024-06/geo1988-copernicusdem-spe-002_producthandbook_i5.0.pdf) states WGS84 horizontal coordinates and EGM2008 (EPSG:3855) vertical reference. The [public COG documentation](https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/readme.html) explicitly calls it a **digital surface model** that includes buildings and vegetation. It is a modern context candidate, not accepted bare-earth or 1890 ground. Access and tile coverage for central Guangzhou still need confirmation; no tile has been imported.

An [official Guangzhou archaeological notice](https://wglj.gz.gov.cn/xxgk/bmwj/ywxx/wwl/content/post_10742941.html) reports an excavation at Guangta Road/Chashu Lane and provides a report link, but its notice gives no surveyed ground elevation or datum. Excavation layers spanning Han to Republican periods cannot be converted into a Qing ground level without a referenced section and height. A [Guangzhou Museum account](https://www.guangzhoumuseum.cn/website_cn/Web/Resource/findingsDetail.aspx?id=53) describes Guangta's present base as 1.8 m below the mosque ground; that is a **relative modern observation**, not an absolute historical elevation.
