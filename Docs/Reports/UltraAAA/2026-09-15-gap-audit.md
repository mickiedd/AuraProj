# UltraAAA gate gap audit — 2026-09-15

This is the pre-edit reading of the owner-supplied reference sheets against the
current saved renders. The three gaps for each gate are the work priority order.

## Wuxianmen

1. **Massing and setting:** the current model is a low, thin wall with a small
   single pavilion. The sheet calls for a substantial grey-brick waterfront
   gate, full crenellated wall mass, deep arch, quays, boats, and Pearl River
   water in the hero composition.
2. **Silhouette and assembly detail:** the current roof is a repeated strip and
   the wall is a uniform grid. Individual tile ribs/ends, ridge caps, eave
   brackets, arch voussoirs, corner quoins, worn threshold, and a properly
   planked gate door are not reading in silhouette.
3. **Surface age and palette:** the current wall is pale and flat, with no
   convincing lime-plaster remnants, directional water staining, moss, chipped
   brick, or harbour grime. The capture has a black horizon and grey void that
   undercut every material judgement.

## Zhengximen / Great West Gate

1. **Massing and proportions:** the current asset is too small and too cleanly
   separated from its wall wings. The sheet's elevation requires a broad,
   battered gate wall, large central arch, deep pavilion, paired roof levels,
   and a continuous west-barbican relationship.
2. **Silhouette and assembly detail:** the current wall reads as thin repeated
   bricks, while the reference shows courses with block variation, quoins,
   crenellations, a dressed arch ring, individual roof rows, dougong arms, and
   a real timber door with ironwork.
3. **Surface age and capture context:** the current surfaces are near-white and
   unweathered, with no planted wall growth or street context; the black horizon
   and featureless ground make it read like a floating grey test mesh.

## Dadongmen / Great East Gate

1. **Evidence-facing massing:** the current front capture is actually an
   underlit tunnel/door view, so it does not prove the sheet's battered stone
   wall, crenellations, two-storey pavilion, balcony, lattice windows, or
   three-quarter river-side approach.
2. **Silhouette and material separation:** the sheet requires individually
   legible tile rows, eave brackets, balcony rails, lattice, arch voussoirs,
   and a planked door. Stone, plaster, wood, iron, and vegetation must remain
   distinct; the current close render exposes crude detached vegetation shards
   and overly regular masonry.
3. **Historical weathering and scene:** the reference evidence is heavy stone
   staining, moss/plant growth, dark water lines, chipped edges, and a real
   ground/water approach. The current harness is black and void-like instead.

## Guidemen / Guide Gate

1. **Massing and proportions:** the current gate is a narrow, low preview with
   shallow roof projection; the sheet requires a broad masonry arch wall, deep
   two-tier pavilion, strong front/rear continuity, balcony rails, and the
   surviving inscription/plaque relationship.
2. **Silhouette and detail:** the current roof, bracket line, arch, parapet,
   door, and wall are mostly smooth or repeated. The reference requires roof
   tile rows and ends, separate bracket blocks, chipped arch stones,
   crenellations, door studs/iron ring, and readable stone inscription.
3. **Age, vegetation, and lighting:** the current material is an olive flat
   slab with almost no plaster/stone variation; it lacks the sheet's stains,
   moss, plants, aged timber, and street-side contrast. The `RealismUpgrade`
   subfolder was inspected and is currently empty, so nothing there may be
   discarded while the upgrade is rebuilt.

## Zhengnanmen / Great South Gate

1. **Correct lineage and massing:** the current V3 preview is a near-clay,
   three-tier block presentation; the supplied HighFidelity sheet requires the
   HighFidelity V2 actor asset with three storeys, green glazed roof systems,
   ornamental ridges, and the documented 25.6 m by 22.5 m proportions.
2. **Silhouette and authored detail:** the current render shows giant planar
   roof surfaces and sparse ornament. The sheet requires glazed tile ends and
   ridge ornaments, individually articulated dougong, red timber columns and
   lattice, arch voussoirs, a worn stone/plaster base, and a real studded door.
3. **Lineage-specific material and proof:** the HighFidelity materials listed
   by the owner (`M_GlazedTile_Green`, `M_Gold_RidgeOrnament`,
   `M_Signboard_Zhengnanmen`, `M_Stone_Aged`, `M_Wood_DarkAged`,
   `M_Wood_RedLacquer`, `M_DarkInterior`, plus `N_ArchFill_*`) are not what the
   current V3 preview proves. They need six-map, non-constant authored graphs,
   raking-light depth proof, and matched front/close/rear evidence from the
   HighFidelity preview map.

## Priority rule

For every gate, work in the order: correct massing/silhouette, then
silhouette-level assemblies, then authored surface ageing and the capture
environment. A manifest without personally inspected captures is not a pass.
