## Intent

Expand the existing easy-English retelling so every chapter reaches at least 3,000 words, then review the complete manuscript for duplicates, continuity bugs, and suitability for a ten-year-old reader.

## Changed content

- Expanded all 19 chapters in [a-little-princess-simple-english.md](../../Books/a-little-princess-simple-english.md) with original scenes, dialogue, gentle humor, concrete action, and clearer emotional transitions.
- Removed four accidental repeated scene blocks created during expansion.
- Reworked Chapter 14 so the roof sequence has one preparation scene and one actual midnight crossing; the gifts then arrive once and flow cleanly into Chapter 15’s Magic.
- Kept the retelling’s story guard: imagination supports Sara’s endurance, while friendship and practical kindness—not wealth—solve the central problem.
