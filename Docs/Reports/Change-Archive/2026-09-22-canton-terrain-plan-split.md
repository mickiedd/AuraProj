# Canton terrain plan daily-document split

## Intent

Convert the reviewed 20-working-day master plan into independently executable daily work packages without losing its cross-day contracts.

## Changed behavior

- Added 20 daily execution documents under `Docs/Plans/Canton-Terrain-Implementation/`.
- Added one paired resource/evidence sheet for each day under `Resources/`.
- Every daily plan contains its entry gate, original work/deliverables/completion gate, evidence requirement, stop/go rule and next-day handoff.
- Every resource sheet records required inputs, expected artifacts, evidence checklist and a structured completion record.
- Added navigation from both the master plan and the daily-plan index.

## Validation

- Confirmed exactly 20 daily plans and 20 resource sheets.
- Checked day/resource, previous-day and next-day links.
- Confirmed every daily plan contains Goal, Entry gate, Required evidence, Stop/go rule and Handoff sections.
- Confirmed every resource sheet contains inputs, expected artifacts, evidence checklist and completion record.
