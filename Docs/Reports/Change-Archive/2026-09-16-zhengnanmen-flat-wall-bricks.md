# Zhengnanmen V2 flat wall brick correction

The user requested continued repair of walls whose bricks were not tiled flat. Inspection of the exact packed V2 ActorAsset and source GLB confirmed all 120,000 triangles in `GEO_StoneBlock_High` face inward. The material is single sided, so outside cameras saw rear/internal surfaces, making stones look like recessed boxes. Repeated blocks also had randomized widths, heights and depths.

Replaced only the wall stone HISM's mesh with a task-owned, outward-wound closed 44-triangle chamfered stone prototype. Six major faces are flat; a small 0.6 cm prototype bevel becomes a restrained edge break at instance scale. Aligned original source cells into 23 level courses on each of four coplanar wall faces, with 1.2 cm bed/perpendicular joints. Original stack-bond arrangement retained. Arch-adjacent cells were shortened to clear the opening; four cells fully inside the arch were removed. New wall count: 4,150; complete gate: 114 components / 12,383 instances. Kept current stone material, source rotation, component collision configuration, and all 113 other components. New mesh has its own automatically generated collision; original mesh and source GLB remain untouched. No map saved or loaded.
