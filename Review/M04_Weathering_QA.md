# M04 vegetation and localized wetness QA — provisional blockout

The district contains 38 collision-disabled, deterministic green weed proxies and 14 small damp-ground patches. The 40-point weed sequence excludes two positions near the mixed-lane crossing. The authored offsets keep growth outside the principal paving, gutter and gate opening; `Data/Foliage_SpawnRules.json` and `QA/Vegetation_Exclusions.md` record the fallback placement rule. Day 01 PCG preflight did not pass, so ordinary actors were used.

`QA/Canton_District/dry_ground.png` and `after_rain_ground.png` are fixed-camera renders from the same loaded map with only the damp patches toggled. The closer camera exposes one representative patch clearly; `QA/Canton_District/Capture_QA.json` confirms identical viewpoint and 38,915 changed pixels at an eight-level RGB threshold. All 14 cards were moved to a 0.5 cm bottom offset, thinned to 1 cm, made non-shadow-casting and changed to a dark neutral swatch after visual review found a pale floating rectangle. The saved actor/material changes are recorded in `QA/Canton_District/Wetness_Refinement.json`. This proves the wetness layer is local and separable; it is not a rainfall or hydrology simulation. The patches follow the engineering road-edge corridor and the three diagnostic gutter catchments, which are not documented Qing drains. The native reload result records instance counts and 100 road collision hits.

**Decision: M04 INCOMPLETE / PROVISIONAL.** Sparse placement and dry/wet toggling pass only as an implementation blockout. The green cubes are not vegetation assets; no period species, debris source, authentic litter distribution, final roughness/normal treatment or draw-call benchmark is accepted. Native reload counts 38 weed proxy actors and 14 wet cards, but a runtime instance-count and draw-call snapshot was not captured. No reduction of the Day 18–19 scope was approved. Final M04 art and its performance evidence remain open in `Data/Open_Issues.csv`.
