# Vrooman/Kerr map scale inspection — 2026-09-24

Source: `MAP-001`, the British Library scan included in the [1880 second edition of *The Canton Guide*](https://www.flickr.com/photos/britishlibrary/11135383854/). The scan is 7017 × 4384 pixels. The reproducible crop `QA/Canton_Map_ScaleBar_Crop.jpg` covers original pixels X 5000–6100 and Y 3700–4300.

The lower-right graphic bar has four alternating divisions but **no legible number or unit** in the source scan. No representative fraction is printed in the visible map margins. A pixel-to-ground ratio inferred from the provisional affine would reuse the very transform whose independent checks fail, so it is not a source scale. The map's numeric scale remains **unknown**.

This is a documented source limitation, not a missing transcription. Day 02's source-selection deliverable can close with this exception; Day 03 still needs ground controls and independent checks. The actual date of Vrooman's underlying survey is also unverified. Do not label the 1880 publication date as the survey date.
