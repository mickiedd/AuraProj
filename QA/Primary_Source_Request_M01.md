# Primary-source request — horizontal and vertical M01 evidence

## Horizontal H1/H2 request

Please provide the highest-resolution/original survey or conservation drawing for the named Canton feature/district, including:

- coordinate reference system and horizontal datum;
- exact point definitions (corner, tower centre, benchmark, monument survey point, etc.);
- survey date and method;
- scale/grid/control marks or vector/CAD/GIS coordinates;
- expected horizontal accuracy or source survey class;
- enough persistent features for independent fit and check sets.

For H1, the project needs at least five fit controls and eight disjoint city holdouts, with at least two holdouts in each quadrant and both perimeter and interior coverage. The acceptance budget is RMSE ≤15 m and worst residual ≤30 m.

For H2, select the local district by named historical feature and evidence coverage. The project needs at least four independent local checks from a local survey, conservation or archaeological network and must test against RMSE ≤2 m and worst residual ≤4 m, or the stricter half-feature-width rule. Do not use the current failed-transform-derived UTM squares as accepted survey bounds.

The initial named requests are for **文明门 (Wenmingmen)**, primary, and **正东门 / 大东门 (Zhengdongmen)**, fallback. Please identify the surveyed historic gate/threshold footprint and persistent surrounding features, rather than using the approximate modern road intersection as a measured point.

## Vertical request

For any late-Qing (1880–1900) walking/ground surface, please provide:

- exact excavation section/point identifier and plan coordinates;
- archaeological interpretation and dating basis;
- absolute surveyed elevation of the surface, not only depth below modern grade;
- benchmark identifier and vertical datum/realization;
- survey/level method and uncertainty;
- original section sheet/CAD/table sufficient to reproduce the height.

Relative layer depths or a generic “elevation above sea level” with no datum realization cannot be used for historical acceptance. Modern DTM may support broad morphology only and remains D-confidence/provisional for Historical_Z.
