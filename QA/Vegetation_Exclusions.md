# Provisional urban growth placement

The isolated district uses 38 collision-disabled weed proxies, placed deterministically outside the 6 m principal stone road, its shoulders, and the mixed-lane crossing. Two positions in the 40-point sequence are excluded around the crossing. `Data/Foliage_SpawnRules.json` states the offsets and fallback mode; PCG was not used because the Day 01 PCG preflight did not pass.

The cubes are geometric visibility and exclusion probes, not historically identified plant species or final foliage meshes. The gate opening, stone slabs and drain covers have no weed actors. The placement is D-confidence pending period-specific vegetation evidence and a foliage asset pass.
